# SubSub vs Knowify

**Short answer:** Knowify is a financial tool first: job costing, progress billing and tight QuickBooks integration, popular with trade contractors running their own business. SubSub is for the GC (or property manager or owner) managing a network of subs. Different buyers, mostly.

## At a glance

| | SubSub | Knowify |
|---|---|---|
| Built for | GCs managing subcontractors | Trade contractors managing their finances |
| Sub onboarding and compliance | Yes, enforced | Not the core of the product |
| Crew availability and matching | Yes | No |
| Work orders and change orders | Yes | Contracts and change orders, finance-focused |
| Job costing, progress billing, QuickBooks | No | Yes |
| Labor warranty tracked per job | Yes | No |
| Pricing | Free (3 subs) / $99/mo unlimited users | Tiered subscription |

## Where Knowify wins

- **Job costing and billing** with deep QuickBooks integration.

## Where SubSub wins

- **Managing the sub network**: verified coverage, automatic renewals, crew dispatch, warranty tracking.

## FAQ

**Should a GC use Knowify or SubSub?**
If your problem is costing and billing, Knowify. If it's knowing which subs are covered, free and accountable, SubSub. Some GCs use both.
