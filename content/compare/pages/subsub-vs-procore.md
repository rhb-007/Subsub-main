# SubSub vs Procore for small GCs

**Short answer:** Procore is the enterprise standard for commercial construction and the right choice for large GCs. For a small or residential GC whose main headache is managing subs, it's usually more platform, more implementation and more cost than the job needs. SubSub does the sub side and starts free.

## At a glance

| | SubSub | Procore |
|---|---|---|
| Built for | GCs, property managers and owners managing a sub network | Mid-size to enterprise commercial construction |
| Setup | Same day: add a company, send an invite | Implementation project |
| Sub self-onboarding and document upload | Yes, from their phone | Yes, through prequalification workflows |
| Insurance checked against your requirements | Yes | Yes, with compliance and prequalification tools |
| Non-compliant subs blocked from assignment | Yes | Configurable |
| Crew-level availability and matching | Yes | No |
| Labor warranty tracked per job | Yes | Not a core feature |
| RFIs, submittals, drawings, quality and safety | No | Yes |
| Pricing | Published: free / $99/mo | Quote-based, priced on annual construction volume |

## Where Procore wins

- **Commercial project depth.** RFIs, submittals, drawings, quality, safety, financials and bidding in one platform.
- **Scale.** Built for big teams, many projects and owner reporting requirements.
- **Ecosystem.** If your owners, architects and big subs already live in Procore, that matters.

## Where SubSub wins for small GCs

- **Cost.** $99/month for unlimited subs, users and jobs vs volume-based enterprise pricing.
- **Time to value.** Invite your subs today; they build their own profiles.
- **Built for residential and service work.** Trades, ZIP-radius coverage, crew availability and work orders that fit roofing, siding, remodeling and property turns.
- **Labor warranty tracking** per job, with callbacks vs warranty claims separated.

## Which should you choose?

- **Choose Procore** if you're a commercial GC running complex projects with formal document control, or your clients require it.
- **Choose SubSub** if you're a small or residential GC, property manager or owner who needs to know who's covered, who's free and whose warranty applies, without an enterprise rollout.

## FAQ

**Is Procore worth it for a small general contractor?**
Sometimes, if your work is commercial and document-heavy. For residential and small GCs focused on managing subs, a lighter tool usually fits better.

**How much does Procore cost?**
Procore doesn't publish pricing. It's quoted based on annual construction volume, with unlimited users.

**What's a cheaper alternative to Procore for subcontractor management?**
SubSub is free for up to 3 subs and $99/month for unlimited subs and users.
