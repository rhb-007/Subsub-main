# SubSub vs Fieldwire

**Short answer:** Fieldwire is excellent at field coordination: plans, tasks, punch lists and inspections. It doesn't manage the subcontractor relationship. SubSub handles who's approved, who's covered, who's free and whose warranty applies. They solve different problems.

## At a glance

| | SubSub | Fieldwire |
|---|---|---|
| Built for | Managing a subcontractor network | Field task and plan management |
| Sub onboarding and document collection | Yes | No |
| Insurance and license compliance | Yes, enforced (license checked against the Washington L&amp;I registry; elsewhere the number is recorded on the record) | No |
| Crew availability and matching | Yes | No |
| Work orders subs accept or decline | Yes | Tasks assigned to users |
| Plans, punch lists, inspections | No | Yes |
| Labor warranty tracked per job | Yes | No |
| Pricing | Free (3 subs) / $99/mo unlimited users | Per user, with a limited free plan |

## Where Fieldwire wins

- **Drawings and punch lists** in the field, with markup and version control.
- **Task-level coordination** across trades on site.

## Where SubSub wins

- **Everything before the crew shows up**: onboarding, insurance verification, compliance, matching and the work order.
- **Everything after**: change orders, callbacks and labor warranty claims.

## FAQ

**Can I use SubSub and Fieldwire together?**
Yes. SubSub decides who's approved and assigned; Fieldwire coordinates the work on site.
