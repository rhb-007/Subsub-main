# SubSub vs Contractor Foreman

**Short answer:** Contractor Foreman packs a lot of modules into a low price. SubSub does one thing in depth: managing your subcontractors. If you want breadth on a budget, Contractor Foreman. If sub compliance, dispatch and warranty are where you lose time and money, SubSub.

## At a glance

| | SubSub | Contractor Foreman |
|---|---|---|
| Built for | Managing a subcontractor network | Broad, budget construction management |
| Subs self-onboard and maintain their own documents | Yes | Limited |
| Insurance checked against your requirements | Yes | Basic tracking |
| Non-compliant subs blocked from assignment | Yes | No |
| Crew-level availability and matching | Yes | No |
| Labor warranty tracked per job | Yes | No |
| Estimating, time tracking, daily logs, financials | No | Yes |
| Pricing | Free (3 subs) / $99/mo unlimited users | Low published tiers |

## Where Contractor Foreman wins

- **Breadth for the price.** Many modules for a small contractor's budget.

## Where SubSub wins

- **Depth on subs**: subs keep their own records, coverage is verified against your requirements, renewals are chased automatically.
- **Enforcement**: a sub who isn't clear can't be given the job.
- **Unlimited users** on one flat plan.

## FAQ

**Is SubSub a Contractor Foreman alternative?**
For managing subcontractors, yes. For estimating, time tracking and financials, no.
