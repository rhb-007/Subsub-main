# SubSub vs JobTread

**Short answer:** JobTread is a well-liked all-in-one for estimating, budgeting, scheduling and invoicing, with transparent pricing. SubSub focuses on the subcontractor relationship: verifying insurance, keeping compliance current, matching available crews and tracking labor warranties. Different jobs; they work well together.

## At a glance

| | SubSub | JobTread |
|---|---|---|
| Built for | Managing a subcontractor network | Running jobs from estimate to invoice |
| Free sub/vendor access | Yes | Yes, free portals |
| Insurance checked line by line against your requirements | Yes | Not the core of the product |
| Automatic expiry reminders (email + SMS) | Yes | Not the core of the product |
| Non-compliant subs blocked from assignment | Yes | No |
| Matching by trade, distance, crew availability, rating | Yes | No |
| Labor warranty tracked per job | Yes | No |
| Estimating, budgets, Gantt, invoicing, CRM | No | Yes |
| Pricing | Free (3 subs) / $99/mo, unlimited users | $199/mo + $20 per internal user ($159 + $18 annual) |

## Where JobTread wins

- **All-in-one job management** with strong estimating and budgeting.
- **Transparent pricing** and highly rated support.
- **Scheduling.** Users regularly praise its Gantt chart.

## Where SubSub wins

- **Compliance enforcement**, not just document storage.
- **Crew-level dispatch** by trade, distance, availability and rating.
- **Labor warranties** tracked per job.
- **Flat price.** Unlimited internal users on Scale, so the bill doesn't move when the office grows.

## Can you use both?

Yes. Run the job and the budget in JobTread, and the sub network in SubSub. On Scale, connect via Zapier, Make, n8n or API so scheduled jobs arrive in SubSub automatically.

## FAQ

**Is SubSub a JobTread alternative?**
Only for the subcontractor side. JobTread covers estimating, budgets and invoicing; SubSub doesn't.

**Which is cheaper for a growing team?**
SubSub's Scale plan is $99/month with unlimited users. JobTread charges per internal user, which is efficient for small teams and grows with headcount.
