# SubSub vs Projul

**Short answer:** Projul is an all-in-one construction management suite with flat pricing and no per-user fees. SubSub is a dedicated system for the subcontractor relationship. If you want one tool for the business, Projul. If you need sub compliance, dispatch and warranty tracking done properly, SubSub.

## At a glance

| | SubSub | Projul |
|---|---|---|
| Built for | Managing a subcontractor network | Running a contracting business end to end |
| Pricing model | Free (3 subs) / $99/mo flat | Flat-rate tiers, no per-user fees |
| Starting paid price | $99/mo | Around $4,788/yr on published plans |
| Insurance checked against your requirements | Yes | Not the core of the product |
| Non-compliant subs blocked from assignment | Yes | No |
| Crew-level availability and matching | Yes | No |
| Labor warranty tracked per job | Yes | No |
| CRM, estimating, scheduling, invoicing | No (connects to your CRM) | Yes |

## Where Projul wins

- **One system** for CRM, estimating, scheduling and invoicing.
- **No per-user fees**, which suits growing offices.

## Where SubSub wins

- **Depth on subs**: line-by-line insurance checks, automatic renewal chasing, hard blocks on non-compliant subs.
- **Crew-level dispatch** and work orders subs accept from their phone.
- **Labor warranty tracking** per job.
- **Lower entry price**, with a free plan.

## FAQ

**Can I use SubSub with Projul?**
Yes. Keep the business in Projul and the sub network in SubSub. SubSub's Scale plan connects through Zapier, Make, n8n or API.
