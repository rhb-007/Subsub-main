From work order to closed unit.

A make-ready across three trades, dispatched and documented.

  1. Add the unit as a job

    Address, unit number, square footage, access notes, target date. Pick the trades: paint, flooring, plumbing.

  2. Match on coverage and proximity

    Only vendors whose insurance is verified and whose coverage area reaches the property appear as available.

  3. Issue a work order per trade

    Each vendor gets their own scope and price, numbered and dated, with any photos or measurements attached.

  4. They accept and schedule

    Accepted on their phone, or booked straight in if you've enabled auto-schedule for vendors you trust.

  5. Close and rate

    Mark the unit complete, rate each crew, and the record stays attached to the property and the vendor.

Built for the way portfolios actually run.

Multiple properties, recurring vendors, constant turnover of small jobs.

  • Requirements you set once. Your coverage minimums apply to every vendor, and each certificate is checked against them line by line.
  • Workers' comp isn't skipped. It's an explicit confirmation on the review, alongside the state licence check.
  • Accept a lower limit deliberately. A handyman on a $400 repair may not carry $2M. Accept it with a written reason, recorded against your name.
  • Reminders that don't need you. Expiring certificates trigger their own email and text, pointing the vendor at their portal to upload.
  • Unlimited users on Scale. Every property manager on your team works from the same vendor list.
