A three-trade job, start to finish.

Roof, siding and gutters on the same house, three different subs, one job record.

  1. Enter the job once

    Address, square footage, stories, start date, materials source and your measurements. Tick roofing, siding and gutters.

  2. Get ranked matches per trade

    For each trade, the subs who cover it, can reach the ZIP, have a crew free that day and are compliant.

  3. Assign a crew and issue the work order

    Add that trade's scope and the sub's pay, and the work order is generated. Three trades, three work orders.

  4. They accept from their phone

    Or with auto-schedule on, it books straight onto their calendar and you skip the round trip.

  5. Close it out and rate each crew

    Ratings are per crew and roll up to the company, so next time the ranking already knows who did well.

Details that matter on a live job.

The things that catch you out on a Tuesday morning.

  • Crew-level availability. A sub with two crews isn't simply 'busy' — you see which crew is free and assign that one.
  • Coverage by city or radius. Some subs work named cities, others travel a set distance from a ZIP. Both are handled, and matching respects it.
  • Measurements travel with the work order. Upload the takeoff once and every crew on the job has it attached to theirs.
  • Signed work orders come back in. Upload the countersigned copy against the work order it belongs to.
  • Your own sign-in address. Subs log in at yourcompany.subsub.work with your logo on it, not ours.
