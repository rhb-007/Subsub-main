SubSub vs Knowify

Short answer: Knowify is a financial tool first: job costing, progress billing and tight QuickBooks integration, popular with trade contractors running their own business. SubSub is for the GC (or property manager or owner) managing a network of subs. Different buyers, mostly.

FAQ

Should a GC use Knowify or SubSub? If your problem is costing and billing, Knowify. If it's knowing which subs are covered, free and accountable, SubSub. Some GCs use both.
